# ADR-0005 — Honest labelling of the "neuro" mechanisms

* Status: accepted · 2026-09-25

## Decision
"Cortisol", "dopamine", "tunnel vision" and "Free Energy" are **names for latent control
variables inspired by** published findings (stress narrows attention; reward-prediction
error; Einstellung / set effects in chess; surprisal). CCA does **not** claim to simulate
physiology, and no source links a Lorenz attractor to human temperament. Every mechanism
must state a *falsifiable behavioural prediction* that the benchmark suite can reject
(see `docs/science.md`). Hand-set parameters are labelled "not fitted".
